Delete your account

DigiGaan

This page permanently deletes your DigiGaan account and everything synced to it. You do not need the app installed to use it.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (email, name, and profile picture held by DigiGaan)
  • Your favorite songs
  • Your recently played songs
  • The record of devices signed in to your account

What cannot be deleted here

Anonymous crash reports and aggregate usage statistics are not linked to your account, so they cannot be looked up or removed individually. They are not identifiable as yours and expire automatically — crash reports within 90 days, analytics within 14 months. See the privacy policy for detail.

Delete now

Sign in with the Google account you use in DigiGaan. Signing in here is only used to verify it is you, and the account is erased immediately afterwards.

This cannot be undone. Your account and all synced data will be permanently removed.

Prefer to email us?

You can also request deletion by emailing digigaan@gmail.com from your account address with the subject "Delete my account". We complete these within 30 days.
