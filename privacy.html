DigiGaan · Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what information the DigiGaan mobile app collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have. It applies to the DigiGaan app on all platforms where it is distributed.

1. Who we are

DigiGaan ("we", "us") develops and publishes the DigiGaan app. If you have questions about this policy or about your data, contact us at REPLACE_ME@example.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

DigiGaan does not require you to create an account. If you contact us for support, we receive the email address you write from and whatever you choose to include in your message.

Information collected automatically

The app and the third-party services it uses may collect limited technical information:

  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, app version, and language setting.
  • Usage and diagnostic data — which screens are opened, crashes, and performance data, used to find and fix problems.
  • Advertising identifiers — a resettable advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS) may be used by our advertising partner to serve and measure ads. You can reset or limit this identifier in your device settings.
  • Approximate location — derived from IP address, used by ad partners for regional ad relevance. We do not collect precise GPS location.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card details.

3. How we use information

  • To operate the app and deliver the features you use.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability and performance.
  • To display advertising, including personalized advertising where you have consented to it.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. Advertising

DigiGaan displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob may use advertising identifiers and other device data to select and measure ads. Google's handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms and in the AdMob documentation.

Opting out of personalized ads. On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads and turn on "Opt out of Ads Personalization". On iOS, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off tracking for DigiGaan. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

Our authorized sellers are declared publicly at digigaan.github.io/app-ads.txt, in line with the IAB Tech Lab app-ads.txt standard.
